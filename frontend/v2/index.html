      Unlock your vault

      In this demo, your MetaMask wallet signs the proof. Enter your separate Dvault recovery key to unlock your files.

      Forgot your recovery key?

      Saved files still need the original Dvault key. To test with a separate vault, you can switch accounts in the same MetaMask wallet and stay on the Dvault demo network.

      What stays private?

      Encrypt
      AES-GCM encrypts files, filenames, and version notes in your browser. Keep your separate Dvault recovery key. Dvault cannot reset it.
      Store
      IPFS holds the encrypted file. File retrieval depends on the pin remaining available. Keep a separate backup for files you can’t lose.
      Verify
      The contract appends each version and its ciphertext fingerprint. The wallet address, timing, and storage identifier are public. The app compares encrypted bytes with the recorded fingerprint before decrypting.
      Demo network
      The local class demo can be reset by its operator. Use synthetic files for demos. Start on the same wallet and use the same recovery key on each device.
